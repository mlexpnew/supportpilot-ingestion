## Overview
You can request a refund within 30 days of delivery for unused items in their
original packaging.

## Steps
1. Go to My Orders and click Return or refund next to the item.
2. Choose a reason and add a photo if the item is damaged.
3. Print the return label or book a free pickup.
4. We inspect the item within 2 working days of receiving it.

## Still need help?
Some items, such as gift cards and personalised products, cannot be refunded.
Contact support if you are unsure.
