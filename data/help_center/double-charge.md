## Overview
Seeing two charges usually means one is a temporary authorisation hold, not a
real payment.

## Steps
1. Wait 3 working days. Pending holds usually disappear on their own.
2. Check My Orders to confirm you placed only one order.
3. Compare the amounts. A hold often shows as pending on your bank app.

## Still need help?
If both charges are still there after 3 working days, contact support with the
order number and the date of each charge. We will refund the duplicate.
