## Overview
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days and express takes 1 to 2.
Weather, holidays and courier delays can occasionally add extra days.

## Steps
1. Check the latest status on your tracking page.
2. Look for a missed-delivery card or a message from the courier.
3. Ask neighbours or your building reception if a parcel was left with them.

## Still need help?
If your parcel is more than 5 working days late, contact support. We will
open a courier investigation and, if it is lost, send a replacement or refund.
