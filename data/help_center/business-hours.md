## Overview
Chat and email are monitored every day. Phone support runs on weekdays.

## Hours (IST)
- Chat and email: 8 AM to 10 PM, every day.
- Phone: 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Friday.
- Public holidays: chat only, with longer reply times.

## Response times
Chat usually replies within 5 minutes. Email replies within 24 hours.

## Still need help?
Messages sent outside these hours are answered first thing the next
morning.
