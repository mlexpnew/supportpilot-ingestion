## Overview
You can cancel an order free of charge until it is dispatched.

## Steps
1. Go to My Orders and open the order.
2. Click Cancel order and choose a reason.
3. You will get a cancellation email, and any payment is refunded
   automatically.

## Already shipped?
Once shipped, the order cannot be cancelled. Refuse the delivery or request a
return after it arrives instead.

## Still need help?
Contact support if the cancel button is missing for an order that has not
shipped yet.
