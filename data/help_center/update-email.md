## Overview
You can change the email address used for login and notifications.

## Steps
1. Go to Account, then Profile.
2. Click Change email and enter the new address.
3. Confirm using the link sent to the new email.
4. We also send an alert to your old email for security.

## Still need help?
If you no longer have access to your old email and cannot log in, contact
support. We will verify your identity before making the change.
