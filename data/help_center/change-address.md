## Overview
You can change the delivery address only before the order is dispatched.
After dispatch, the courier controls the parcel.

## Steps
1. Go to My Orders and open the order.
2. If the status is Processing, click Change address and save the new one.
3. If the status is Shipped, use the courier link to request a redirect.

## Still need help?
If the option is greyed out, contact support within 1 hour of ordering and we
will try to update the address before it ships.
