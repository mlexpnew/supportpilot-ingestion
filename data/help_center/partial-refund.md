## Overview
A partial refund returns part of the order amount instead of the full price.

## When partial refunds happen
- Only some items from a multi-item order were returned.
- An item came back used or without its original packaging.
- A discount or free gift was linked to the returned item.

## How it is calculated
We refund the price you paid for the returned item, minus any discount that
was shared across the order. The breakdown appears in your refund email.

## Still need help?
If the amount looks wrong, reply to the refund email and we will review it.
