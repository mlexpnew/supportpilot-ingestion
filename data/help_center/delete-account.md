## Overview
Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, saved addresses and
order history. This cannot be undone.

## Steps
1. Make sure you have no open orders or active subscriptions.
2. Go to Account, then Privacy, then Delete account.
3. Confirm by entering your password.
4. We complete the deletion within 7 days and email you when it is done.

## Still need help?
Invoices may be kept for legal tax reasons even after deletion. Contact
support if you have questions about what is kept.
