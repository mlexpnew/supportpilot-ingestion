## Overview
We collect only the data needed to process orders, provide support and
improve our service.

## What we collect
- Contact details and delivery addresses.
- Order and payment history (we never store full card numbers).
- Support conversations, used to resolve and improve support quality.

## Your choices
You can download your data, change marketing preferences or delete your
account at any time under Account, then Privacy.

## Still need help?
Contact support for any privacy question or data request.
