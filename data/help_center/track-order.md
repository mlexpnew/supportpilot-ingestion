## Overview
Every order gets a tracking link once it leaves our warehouse. Use it to see
where your parcel is and the expected delivery date.

## Steps
1. Open the shipping confirmation email and click Track your order.
2. Or log in, go to My Orders, and click Track next to the order.
3. The first tracking scan can take up to 24 hours to appear.

## Still need help?
If tracking has not changed for 3 working days, contact support with your
order number and we will check with the courier.
