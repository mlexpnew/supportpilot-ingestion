## Overview
You can cancel your subscription at any time. You keep access until the end of
the current billing period.

## Steps
1. Log in and go to Account, then Subscriptions.
2. Click Cancel subscription and confirm.
3. You will receive a confirmation email. No further renewals will be charged.

## Pausing instead
If you only need a break, choose Pause for up to 3 months instead of
cancelling.

## Still need help?
If you were charged after cancelling, contact support with the confirmation
email.
