## Overview
If you forgot your password, you can set a new one using a reset link sent to
your account email.

## Steps
1. On the login page, click Forgot password.
2. Enter your account email and check your inbox, including spam.
3. Click the link within 30 minutes and choose a new password.
4. Use at least 10 characters with a mix of letters and numbers.

## Still need help?
If the email never arrives, your account may use a different address. Contact
support and we will verify your identity.
