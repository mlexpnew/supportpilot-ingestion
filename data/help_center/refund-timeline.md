## Overview
Once a refund is approved, we send it to your original payment method within
1 working day. Your bank then decides when it appears on your statement.

## Typical timelines
- Credit and debit cards: 5 to 7 working days.
- UPI and wallets: 1 to 3 working days.
- Store credit: instant.

## Still need help?
If 10 working days have passed since approval, contact support. We can share
the refund reference number so your bank can trace it.
