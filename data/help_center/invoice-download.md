## Overview
A tax invoice is created for every order once it is shipped.

## Steps
1. Go to My Orders and open the order.
2. Click Download invoice to get a PDF.
3. For a business invoice, add your company name and tax ID under
   Account, then Billing details, before ordering.

## Still need help?
Invoices cannot be edited after they are issued. If details are wrong,
contact support and we will issue a corrected invoice.
