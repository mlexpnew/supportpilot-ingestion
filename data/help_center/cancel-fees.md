## Overview
Most cancellations are free. A fee applies only in a few cases.

## When a fee applies
- Custom or personalised items already in production: 20 percent of the item
  price.
- Annual plans cancelled after 14 days: the unused months are refunded minus
  one month's fee.

## When it is free
- Orders cancelled before dispatch.
- Monthly subscriptions, at any time.

## Still need help?
Contact support before cancelling if you want to know the exact fee for your
order.
